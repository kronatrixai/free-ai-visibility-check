What we check

Seven signals that make a website easier to crawl, understand and evaluate.

The review starts with technical access, then looks at whether the page actually gives people and machines enough clear information to understand the business and its relevance.

01

Crawlability

robots.txt, accessible HTML, working assets, internal links and whether important information can be reached without unnecessary friction.

02

Indexability

Canonical URLs, HTTPS consistency, index directives, sitemap signals and obvious duplicate or conflicting versions of the same page.

03

Entity clarity

Whether the website makes the business name, offer, location, audience, contact route and relationship to its wider brand clear.

04

Useful answers

Whether the page directly answers the real research, comparison, trust and buying questions customers are likely to ask.

05

Trust and proof

Real evidence, transparent claims, clear identity, credible references and reassurance that helps people judge whether the business is worth considering.

06

Structured meaning

Headings, semantic HTML, metadata and schema that support the visible content instead of exaggerating or contradicting it.

07

Mobile and conversion clarity

Whether the page remains readable and useful on smaller screens and gives visitors an obvious next step without unnecessary confusion.

What good looks like

A clear website should answer the important questions without making visitors or crawlers guess.

  • One clear canonical version of every important page.
  • A title and description that match the page's real purpose.
  • A specific H1 and useful answer near the top of the page.
  • Visible business, service and audience information.
  • Real proof and transparent claims rather than manufactured authority.
  • Helpful internal links to deeper information where needed.
  • Schema that agrees with what a visitor can actually see.
  • A working sitemap containing only URLs worth indexing.
  • Fast, accessible mobile presentation.
  • A clear contact or conversion route for interested visitors.

What is an AI visibility check?

An AI visibility check reviews whether a website gives search engines and AI systems clear, crawlable and trustworthy information about the business, its services, evidence and answers to customer questions.

No. No ethical provider can guarantee rankings, AI citations or recommendations. The purpose of the check is to identify practical weaknesses that can reduce a website's ability to be crawled, understood and considered relevant.

What does KRONATRIX check?

It builds on normal SEO rather than replacing it. Technical SEO, useful content, authority, user experience and trustworthy information remain important for both traditional search and AI-assisted discovery.
